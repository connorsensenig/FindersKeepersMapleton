Before You Come In

What to
expect

We review items by appointment only - this lets us give you and your pieces the time and attention they deserve. Here's how to prepare:

✦

Appointments are available Monday through Saturday during store hours.

✦

All items must fit into one white kitchen-sized bag per appointment.

✦

Items must be clean, freshly cleaned, and free of stains, odors, and pet hair.

✦

Items must be complete and functional - no missing parts or broken pieces.

✦

We do not accept personalized items, promotional giveaways, or personal care products.

✦

All items are subject to final approval by staff. Pricing is set at our discretion.

First time consigning? No worries at all - our team will walk you through the entire process at your appointment. Questions beforehand? Just give us a call at (712) 881-0208.
