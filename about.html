Who We Are

More than a
consignment shop.

We are a family‑owned business rooted in a lifelong love of thrift shopping, upcycling, and uncovering hidden treasures.

We are high school sweethearts and happily married for 30 years with two adult children. We made Mapleton, Iowa our permanent home three years ago after visiting and staying connected through our parents, who have been living here for more than 25 years.

Outside of work, we enjoy time with family and friends, traveling, and appreciating God’s beautiful creation.

We are grateful for this opportunity and excited to be an active part of the Mapleton community.

"We wanted to create a place where people come for the treasures and stay for the community."

- The Finders Keepers Family
