Our Story

Find it, Love it, Keep it!

Finders Keepers Mapleton was born from a belief that beautiful, well-made things deserve a second life - and that the right buyer is always out there, waiting to discover them.

We're a family-owned consignment store in the heart of Mapleton. We specialize in carefully selected clothing, accessories, home décor, and unique treasures.
